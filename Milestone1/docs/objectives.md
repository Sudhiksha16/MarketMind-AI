# MarketMind AI – Project Objectives

## 1. Project Goal

MarketMind AI is a Small Business Sales Intelligence Platform designed to help small businesses understand their sales, customers, and inventory using data-driven analytics.

The platform uses historical retail transaction and inventory data to provide useful insights for business decision-making.

---

## 2. Main Objectives

### 2.1 Sales Analysis

Analyze historical sales transactions to understand:

- Total sales and revenue
- Number of transactions
- Product performance
- Sales trends over time
- Category and regional performance

### 2.2 Inventory Management

Monitor inventory information to help businesses:

- Identify available stock
- Identify products with low stock
- Understand units sold and units ordered
- Compare inventory levels with expected demand
- Improve inventory planning

### 2.3 Customer Analysis

Analyze customer purchasing behaviour to understand:

- Purchase frequency
- Purchase quantity
- Customer spending
- Customer activity
- Customer groups with similar behaviour

### 2.4 Sales Forecasting

Use historical sales data to estimate future sales and demand.

This can help small businesses plan:

- Future inventory requirements
- Sales targets
- Purchasing decisions
- Business resources

### 2.5 Customer Segmentation

Group customers based on their purchasing behaviour so that businesses can better understand different customer groups.

### 2.6 Business Decision Support

Combine sales, customer, and inventory information to provide meaningful insights through an easy-to-use dashboard.
